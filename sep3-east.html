5 · Diamond Beach / Fellsfjara

1.1 km · about 5m

Allow 30–45 minutes. Use the restroom at the lagoon first, then drive to the south-side beach lot. There is no verified toilet at the beach. Never walk across the Route 1 bridge; the marked pedestrian route is under it.

Hold the child and stay well back from the water. Never climb onto the ice, especially near the sea; unexpected waves reach farther than they look.

Restrooms, food and water

  • Orkan Freysnes: verified toilet, fuel, food and coffee. This is the best mid-route service stop.
  • Jökulsárlón: flush toilets plus café/food; café and tours normally 09:00–19:00 in September.
  • Diamond Beach: no verified toilet—use the lagoon facilities first.
  • Viking Café: café/ticket counter 07:30–22:00; public toilet not verified.
  • Olís Höfn: toilets, shop and hot food.

Water: yes—refill both bottles from a cold tap at Freysnes, Jökulsárlón café or Olís Höfn. Let it run until cold. Icelandic potable cold water is safe; do not fill from roadside rivers or waterfalls.

Why this safety verdict is different

SafeTravel's dangerous-glacier notice names Sólheimajökull. Vatnajökull National Park lists Jökulsárlón as accessible year-round, staffed by rangers daily, and currently shows no Jökulsárlón closure. This does not remove ordinary cold-water, moving-ice or beach-wave hazards.
